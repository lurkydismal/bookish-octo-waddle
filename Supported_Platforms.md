hardware architectures and operating system versions.
These combinations are called _platforms_.

bookish-octo-waddle categorizes platforms as follows:

* **Supported**: The platforms and versions that the bookish-octo-waddle team supports.
* **CI-tested**: The bookish-octo-waddle team tests these platforms on every commit.
* **Unsupported**: The bookish-octo-waddle team doesn't test or support these platforms.
